# What makes generative AI different?

::callout{style="c"}
Many types of AI have been used in technology, companies and governments for years.
::

Recently, generative AI has introduced more people than ever with to a hands-on way to use AI. We can **interact with it through natural conversation** and it can **generate convincing text, images or sounds** in response.

::callout{style="b"}
This creates new opportunities and risks for government, which is why some of this training focuses on generative AI specifically.
::

::figure{layout="text aside"}
![](<dta/scormcontent/assets/Virtual Assistant Chatbot.jpg>)

You might encounter generative AI within everyday tools and technology, but it may not always be as obvious as a chatbot.
::

You might use it in software that lets you:

- type a conversational instruction instead of picking settings and options
- press a button to generate unique text or images.
