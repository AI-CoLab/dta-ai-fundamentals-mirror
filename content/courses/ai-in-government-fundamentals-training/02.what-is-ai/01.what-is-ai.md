# What is AI?

::callout{style="c"}
Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of technologies used to make predictions or perform tasks that would normally require a person.
::

::gallery{layout="three column"}
![](<dta/scormcontent/assets/Machine Learning.jpg>)

![](<dta/scormcontent/assets/Computer Vision.jpg>)

![](<dta/scormcontent/assets/Language processing.jpg>)
::

AI usually overlaps enough with human intelligence to be useful, even though they learn and work in different ways. These are some common terms you might have heard before.

::accordion
:::accordion-item
### Machine learning

Techniques and technology which allow computer networks to find and learn from patterns in data. It has a significant role in most AI fields.
:::

:::accordion-item
### AI models

A computer program that was trained on patterns in data so it can make predictions or perform tasks.
:::

:::accordion-item
### Computer vision

Techniques to identify patterns and make predictions about real-world objects as seen through images and video.
:::

:::accordion-item
### Natural language processing

Techniques to process, interpret and respond to written or spoken human language.
:::

:::accordion-item
### Generative AI

A type of machine learning that can generate new and convincing text, images or sounds from conversational prompts.
:::

:::accordion-item
### Training data

The starting data which an AI model learns prediction capabilities from. Flawed or unrepresentative training data may embed biases into the model.
:::
