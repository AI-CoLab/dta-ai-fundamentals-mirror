# Where to find advice

::callout{style="c"}
It's okay if you need a refresh down the track.
::

::callout{style="b"}
There's one easy thing to remember: if you're about to use AI start by **checking advice from your agency and government.**
::

### Follow your agency's advice first

Government agencies and their staff are ultimately responsible for how they adopt AI. Always follow your agency’s advice and policies. Ask for help from the relevant internal teams if you need it.

### Check in with whole-of-government advice

Your agency has to align with the whole-of-government approach to AI, so you can always check in with:

- whole-of-government guidance, such as the Australian Government's [Guidance for using generative AI](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/resources#:~:text=Generative%20AI%20guidance)

- government expectations such as Australia’s [Public Service Values](https://www.apsc.gov.au/working-aps/information-aps-employment/aps-values) and [Code of Conduct](https://www.apsc.gov.au/working-aps/integrity/integrity-resources/code-of-conduct)

- AI ethics frameworks such as [Australia’s AI Ethics Principles](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/australias-artificial-intelligence-ethics-principles/australias-ai-ethics-principles).

To quickly find many of these resources in the Australia context, visit [digital.gov.au/ai/resources](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/resources).
