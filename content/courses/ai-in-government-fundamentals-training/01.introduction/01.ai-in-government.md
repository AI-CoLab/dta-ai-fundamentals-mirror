# AI in government

For decades, government has used Artificial Intelligence (AI) to perform specialised and general tasks, such as:

- investigating patterns of fraud
- providing information through virtual assistants
- digitising and organising physical documents.

Recent advances, including generative AI create new opportunities and challenges. Governments must build and maintain public trust to embrace these opportunities.
