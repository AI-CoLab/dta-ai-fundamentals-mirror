# Why does this matter to you?

::callout{style="c"}
The community expects government to be an exemplar of the responsible use of AI.
::

We need to meet this expectation when we choose to use AI in our work.

This training will give you a basic understanding of AI and how to use it responsibly. By completing this course, you will be:

1. **informed** on the applications and risks of AI
2. **capable** of applying relevant advice
3. **confident** about when it's suitable to use generative AI
4. **empowered** to apply your own judgement with AI.
