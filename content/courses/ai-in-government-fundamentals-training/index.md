# AI in government: fundamentals training

This training module will provide you with a foundational knowledge of AI, the principles of safe and responsible use, and how to put these to work as government staff.

This training module was developed for and by the Australian Government to support the implementation of the [Policy for the responsible use of AI in government](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/policy). The module therefore refers to Australian specific resources that may not be applicable to your jurisdiction.
