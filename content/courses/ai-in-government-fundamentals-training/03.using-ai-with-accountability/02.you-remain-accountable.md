# You remain accountable

::callout{style="c"}
We’re individually accountable for how we use AI and its outputs in our work.
::

## Golden rules

### To remain accountable for your use of AI, you must:

- explain, justify and own your advice and decisions
- assume prompts and supporting information could become public
- ensure personal, classified or sensitive information isn’t inappropriately used or revealed.

### Apply the golden rules whenever you evaluate how to use AI in your work, such as when you’re deciding:

- if AI is appropriate for a task
- if it's the most effective tool for the task
- what to use in your prompts
- how to use the resulting outputs.
