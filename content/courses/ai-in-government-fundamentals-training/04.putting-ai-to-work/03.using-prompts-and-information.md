# Using prompts and information

::callout{style="c"}
From training to prompting, what you get out of an AI tool is only as good as what goes in.
::

Consciously consider the **quality** and **integrity** of your prompts and supporting information, such as documents or datasets, which you input into AI tools.

::tabs
:::tab
### Prompt with quality

Take the time to ensure you provide an AI tool with:

- simple, clear and specific prompts
- essential or relevant supporting information
- robust, complete and up-to-date files or datasets.
:::

:::tab
### Prompt with integrity

Work with colleagues and subject matter experts to:

- counter biases and assumptions in your own prompts and the data you use
- prevent personal, classified, sensitive or copyright-protected information being revealed
- ensure your prompt and supporting information is fair, accurate and relevant.
:::
